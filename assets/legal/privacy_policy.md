# Privacy Policy
Palm Tunes, test version 1.0.0. Last updated: November 4, 2026.

Palm Tunes is a desktop music and video player that is still in development. This version is shared with testers so feedback and criticism can be collected. This page explains, in plain language, what the app does with your data.

## The short version
- There are no accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking in this version.
- The app does not send anything about you to the developer.
- Your camera is used only for hand gestures and for Preview Cam, and only while one of them is on. Camera pictures are processed on your own PC and are never saved or sent anywhere.
- Everything the app remembers (settings, lists, downloads) stays on your computer.

## Camera and hand gestures
- The camera is off by default. It turns on only when you switch on **Camera gestures** in Settings, or when you open **Preview Cam**.
- With gestures on, each camera picture is analysed on your PC by a hand-tracking model (MediaPipe) that runs locally. It looks for hands and hand shapes (for example a thumbs up or a swipe) and turns them into simple commands such as play, pause, next, previous, mute or volume.
- Camera pictures are not recorded, saved to disk, uploaded or shared. They are thrown away right after they are analysed. The app does not look for faces and does not identify people.
- **Preview Cam** shows the live camera picture inside the app window so you can see what the camera sees, with your hands outlined. The picture only passes between parts of the app on your PC to be displayed. It is not stored.
- If Preview Cam is open while Camera gestures are off, the camera is on only for as long as the preview is open.
- You choose which camera is used in Settings. The app remembers the name and number of the camera you pick, and nothing else about it.
- Windows may show its own camera-in-use indicator while the camera is on. Turning off Camera gestures and closing Preview Cam turns the camera off.
- Gestures can control playback in the app and your computer's volume.

## System sound level (the pulsing logo)
To make the logo pulse with the beat, the app measures how loud the low (bass) sounds are in whatever Windows is currently playing. Only a loudness number is used. The sound is not recorded, stored or sent anywhere. The microphone is not used.

## What is stored on your device
- Your settings (accent color, playback options, the camera you chose, and similar).
- Your song list and playlists.
- Information about your downloads (titles, authors, picture links, playlists and their order).
- Downloaded audio and video files, and their thumbnail pictures.

## Internet connections
- Palm Tunes connects to **YouTube and YouTube Music**, and to YouTube's picture servers, to search, stream, download and show thumbnails. Those services can see your IP address and what you request, and handle that under their own terms and privacy policies.
- The **Visit Site** button opens the developer's website in your browser.
- The app makes no other connections and sends no usage data to the developer.

## Feedback and bug reports
If you send feedback, you decide what to include. Screenshots and logs can show song titles, folder paths or camera names.

## Your choices
- Turn off Camera gestures and close Preview Cam whenever you like.
- Delete downloads and clear your lists from Settings.
- Delete the app's data folder to remove everything it has stored.

## Changes
This is a test version, so things may change. The date at the top shows when this page was last updated.
